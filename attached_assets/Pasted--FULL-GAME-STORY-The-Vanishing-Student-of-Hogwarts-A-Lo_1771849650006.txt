🧙‍♂️📜 FULL GAME STORY
“The Vanishing Student of Hogwarts”
(A Logical Mystery Adventure)

🏰 Prologue: A Glitch in the Magic
At midnight, deep inside Hogwarts Castle, the Enchanted Student Registry begins to malfunction.
A warning echoes through the halls:
⚠️ “Student detected without identity.”
 ⚠️ “Classes attended. Records missing.”
A student has:
Appeared in class
Left traces in Hogsmeade
Broken time consistency
Yet officially… does not exist.
Fearing misuse of magic or a flaw in the system itself, Professor McGonagall summons a select group of students — YOU.
You are appointed as Junior Investigators for the Department of Mysteries.
Your mission:
Use logic, deduction, and system-level thinking to uncover the Invisible Student before the records erase themselves forever.

🪄 MINI-GAME 1: The Sorting Hat Interface
(Merged ice-breaker + system access)
🎮 Story
Before you can access the magical records, the Sorting Hat blocks the way.
It declares:
“Only minds that understand patterns may enter.”
🎮 Gameplay
Student profile cards float in the air.
Four Hogwarts Houses appear.
Each profile has traits, habits, and strengths.
🖱️ Players classify students into houses by dragging cards.
When enough profiles are logically matched, the Sorting Hat laughs and opens the gate.
🧠 What’s Really Happening
Classification
Feature matching
Rule-based logic
🪄 You’ve just passed a system-level access check — without an exam.

🍺 MINI-GAME 2: The Three Broomsticks Data Reconstruction
(Merged arithmetic + data cleaning)
🎮 Story
Inside the archives, you discover a damaged receipt from the Three Broomsticks.
Madam Rosmerta mutters:
“Someone was here… but the details don’t add up.”
🎮 Gameplay
Receipt fragments are scattered.
Items, prices, and quantities are mismatched.
NPC clues hint at inconsistencies.
🖱️ Players reconstruct the receipt by fixing incorrect or missing data.
When the receipt becomes logically consistent, a hidden detail reveals:
The Invisible Student was present.
🧠 What’s Really Happening
Data consistency
Debugging
Constraint satisfaction
🍻 This feels like fixing broken data, not solving sums.

🔮 MINI-GAME 3: The Pensieve Paradox
(Merged timeline + anomaly detection)
🎮 Story
The trail leads to a Pensieve holding memories of the same evening.
As you observe, something is wrong:
Events overlap
Times contradict each other
A whisper echoes:
“One of these memories cannot be real.”
🎮 Gameplay
Events appear as glowing orbs on a timeline.
Players drag events to restore logical order.
One memory cannot fit anywhere without breaking logic.
🖱️ Players must identify and remove the corrupted memory.
🧠 What’s Really Happening
Conflict detection
Logical sequencing
Identifying impossible states
🔮 This is debugging a system timeline.

👻 MINI-GAME 4: The Invisible Student Detector
(Core mystery level)
🎮 Story
You finally reach the Hogwarts Class Register.
Names, rolls, attendance, magical traces…
One entry behaves strangely:
Present in some records
Missing in others
The parchment trembles:
“This student exists… but is unseen.”
🎮 Gameplay
Players can toggle data layers:
Attendance
Physical presence
Identity
🖱️ By comparing layers, players identify the student who appears without registration.
A chill runs through the room.
The Invisible Student has been found.
🧠 What’s Really Happening
Cross-data validation
Missing records
System inconsistency detection
🔥 This is the emotional peak of the game.

⚖️ FINAL: The Wizengamot Verdict
(No puzzle — pure decision)
🎮 Story
The truth is revealed.
Now the Wizengamot demands your recommendation.
You are given three choices:
📁 Seal the Records – Protect the system, hide the flaw
🪄 Expose the Truth – Reveal the weakness, accept consequences
❌ Erase All Data – Remove the anomaly forever
There is no correct answer.
Your choice determines:
Final score
Ending scene
Position on the House Cup Leaderboard
🧠 What’s Really Happening
Ethical reasoning
Decision trees
Trade-off analysis
